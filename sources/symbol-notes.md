# Symbol Notes

Coding symbols must stay legible under terminal rendering and syntax coloring.

Rules:
- avoid excessive ornament
- keep punctuation compact
- make `;` and `:` easy to distinguish
- keep `{}` readable in dense code
- make `_` sit low enough to not collide visually
- keep `~` and `^` from becoming noise

Ligature candidates later:
- `->`
- `=>`
- `==`
- `!=`
- `<=`
- `>=`
- `===`
- `!==`
